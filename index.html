About A1 Custom Countertops

Local stone fabrication with a focus on precision and premium materials.

We are a family owned and operated business that specializes in custom countertops for kitchens remodels, bathroom renovations, outdoor spaces, and commercial projects. From slab selection to final installation, our team manages every detail to deliver a flawless finish.

Our shop is equipped with modern fabrication tools, and we work with granite, quartz, marble, and specialty stones. Visit our showroom to see slabs in person or browse our online Slab Store to reserve a slab.

Home Improvement and Remodeling

We also provide home renovation services, including but not limited to, painting, tiling, and plumbing, to help you complete your project with one trusted team.
